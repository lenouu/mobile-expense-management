# Mobile Expense Management Platform

A mobile expense management platform designed to help users plan, record, monitor, and visualize their expenses by category.

The project is developed using an Agile/Scrum approach, with short iterations, continuous collaboration, testing, and progressive delivery of working features.

---

##  Project Overview

Managing personal expenses can become difficult when spending is not properly planned or monitored.

This project aims to provide a simple mobile solution that allows users to:

- Plan expenses by category
- Define budgets for different categories
- Record expenses with relevant details
- Track spending over time
- Monitor spending against planned budgets
- Visualize expenses through clear reports and charts

The platform is being developed progressively through Scrum sprints, with each sprint delivering a working increment of the product.

---

##  Product Vision

> To provide individuals with a simple mobile platform that helps them plan, record, monitor, and understand their expenses by category, enabling them to make better financial decisions through clear and actionable spending information.

---

##  Product Goal

Build and progressively deliver a reliable mobile expense-management platform that allows users to establish spending plans, record and monitor expenses, and visualize their financial activity.
